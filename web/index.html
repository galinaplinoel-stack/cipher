Privacy Features

Comprehensive data protection powered by intelligent automation

🔐

Data Encryption

AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with associated data. Protect sensitive information at rest and in transit with military-grade cryptography.

🕵️

PII Anonymization

Automatic detection and anonymization of emails, phone numbers, SSNs, credit cards, and more. Choose mask, hash, or replace strategies.

🗄️

Secret Vault

Encrypted storage for API keys, credentials, and sensitive data. Supports key rotation, tagging, and secure retrieval.

📋

Audit Logs

Complete audit trail of every encrypt, decrypt, anonymize, and vault operation. Query, filter, and export your security history.

📜

GDPR Compliance

Built-in PII detection helps meet GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulation requirements. Scan and redact personal data automatically.

🔗

Secure Sharing

Share encrypted data with full audit trails. Every access is logged, ensuring accountability and traceability for all shared information.
